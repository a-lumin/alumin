01. Campaign Identity & Creative Intent

Campaign: Target Wild Fable — Autumn on Madison

Creative Objective: Transition the week 9 mood board synthesis into concrete, product-specific campaign assets. For Creative Director Pt. 2, we engineered a dedicated campaign logomark bridging Wild Fable's Gen-Z youth apparel with tactile dark academia and Upper East Side collegiate culture. This identity is directly paired with a real-time, interactive Dynamic Hero Engine rendered via procedural canvas and responsive DOM scaffolding.
