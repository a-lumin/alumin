ITERATION METHODOLOGY & PROMPT LOG

HOW CONVERSATIONAL DIRECTIVES RESHAPED THE WORK

Step 01 // Input Sketch & Structural Breakdown GRAPHITE -> BASELINE

"this is a sketch for the promotional poster to a movie called WANDERING THE STARS. The dome is an observation cupola framing outer space nebulae. Protagonist is holding an emissive holographic starmap deck. Two floating alien mascots: a fluffy twin-antenna sprite on the left, and a single-eyed floating creature on the right."

Step 02 // Character Evolution USER PROMPT

"create another iteration, but maybe with a girl in the middle" — Kept the composition intact while introducing a messy ponytail, sci-fi flight jacket patches, and wireless headphones.

Step 03 // Aspect Ratio & Theatrical Poster Packaging USER PROMPT

"make it into a poster form" — Adapted the artwork to vertical 2:3 proportions, lowered cockpit instrumentation buttons, and rendered the billing block at the base.

Step 04 // Clarity & Linework Pass USER PROMPT

"make it clearer" — Enhanced sharpness, contrast, starlight reflection on glass surfaces, and refined facial details.

Step 05 // Perspective Breakthrough (Final) USER PROMPT

"maybe have her look 3/4s of the screen?" — The critical artistic leap. Moving away from the rigid flat profile and turning her face 3/4 toward the viewer gives the poster its signature warmth and emotional weight.
